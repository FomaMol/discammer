> FREQUENTLY_ASKED

Who is this for?

Anyone who uses email, a phone, or a bank account. The early modules assume zero background; the later ones go deep enough to be useful for IT and security teams.

How long will it take?

Around nine hours of focused study end to end. Most people finish over two to three weeks, one module per evening, but you can pace it however suits you.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in the browser. The simulations work on desktop and mobile, and your progress is saved automatically.

Is it really free?

Yes. DiScammer is a public-interest project. We do not sell your data and we do not charge for the curriculum or the certificate.
