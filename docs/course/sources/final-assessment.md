# Final assessment — source and editorial ledger

Checked **2026-10-20** against each live, specific primary-source page. All office, family, shop, transfer and login stories in these lessons are **synthetic**. The source pages support mechanisms and response options; none documents the fictional events or certifies a learner. Page availability at the checked URLs was verified by reading their contents, not inferred from search snippets. The NCSC pages show older publication/review dates but remained accessible; no archived page was substituted.
