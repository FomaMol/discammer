# AI and deepfakes module: source and claim notes

Checked 2026-10-20 against the specific primary pages below. These support educational mechanisms and response routines, not a live threat feed or a claim that every depicted example happened. All six lesson cases and practices are explicitly synthetic except the bounded official Hong Kong account in the video lesson's explanatory body.

| Primary source | Scope and relevant support | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [FTC, Scammers Use Fake Emergencies To Steal Your Money](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/scammers-use-fake-emergencies-steal-your-money) | US consumer advice dated September 2023: a scammer may use a short online audio clip to clone a loved one's voice; urgency and secrecy recur; independently call a known number or another relative. | Does not establish a universal clip-length threshold, a success rate, or a universal family-code guarantee. US reporting route is country-specific. |
| [FBI IC3, Business Email Compromise](https://www.ic3.gov/CrimeInfo/BEC) | US BEC guidance: account-change requests need secondary-channel verification; contact the sending financial institution promptly after a suspected transfer. | Does not promise recall or prescribe another country's banking workflow. |
| [Hong Kong Government, Legislative Council reply, 26 June 2024](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202406/26/P2024062600192p.htm) | Official description of the end-January 2024 purported CFO case: phishing email, **pre-recorded** fabricated video conference, authorisation to transfer to five accounts, later messaging instructions, about HK$200 million reported loss. | Inquiry still ongoing in the dated reply. Do not describe this as a live interactive deepfake, a court finding, or a general loss rate. |
| [NIST, 2024 GenAI Text-to-Text Pilot, published 25 June 2025](https://www.nist.gov/publications/2024-nist-genai-pilot-study-text-text-evaluation-overview-and-results) | Detector performance varied materially by generator and discriminator in a benchmark of human and machine summaries. | Not an evaluation of an individual phishing email, every language, or all AI detectors. |
| [C2PA v2.4 Content Credentials Explainer](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/explainer/Explainer.html) | Signed manifests can bind provenance assertions to a file under a trust model; provenance does not decide factual truth. | Missing credentials do not establish fabrication; a signed staged scene can still mislead. Viewer and workflow support vary. |
| [Google DeepMind, SynthID](https://deepmind.google/models/synthid/) | Vendor description of invisible watermarking for supported generated image, audio, text and video outputs and detection tools. | Product-specific signal, not universal AI detection or proof that a caption is true. Do not send private media to third-party checkers without reason and consent. |
