# Incident-response course source register

Checked 2026-10-20. All linked pages were opened on the issuing organization's site during this authoring pass; none was treated as a live incident feed or a guarantee of reimbursement. The lesson cases and practices are synthetic. National procedures below are not interchangeable.

| Primary source | Use and limit |
| --- | --- |
| [FTC, Recover a hacked email or social media account](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recover-your-hacked-email-or-social-media-account) | Account signals, provider recovery, session revocation, forwarding and contact warnings. US consumer page, live when checked; provider menus vary. |
| [UK NCSC, Hacked accounts](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/section/respond-recover/hacked-accounts) | Recovery sequence, forwarding, contacts and current UK reporting route to Report Fraud or Police Scotland. Live current guidance hub when checked; UK reporting only. |
| [FTC, What to do if you were scammed](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed) | Contact payment provider by payment type; loss may not be recoverable; exposed credentials/device access need separate response. US consumer guidance, live when checked. |
| [FBI IC3, Complaint FAQ](https://www.ic3.gov/Home/FAQ) | IC3 does not accept attachments; retain originals, including email headers and transaction records. This is a US FBI complaint system, but its FAQ permits anyone who believes they were affected to file, including non-US citizens. It does not replace a local report or prompt bank/payment-provider action, and does not promise investigation status. Live FAQ when checked. |
| [CFPB, Unauthorized bank transaction](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-do-i-get-my-money-back-after-i-discover-an-unauthorized-transaction-or-money-missing-from-my-bank-account-en-1017/) | Prompt bank notice and the distinction between US electronic transfer protections and other transaction types. US only; no US deadline is exported to other countries. Live when checked. |
| [Bank of Russia, Unauthorized card debit](https://www.cbr.ru/Reception/TopicalMessage/Page/3402) | Prompt issuer contact, card block, written dispute, police report for an unauthorized Russian card debit. This does not establish rights for a customer-authorized transfer. Live when checked. |
| [FTC, Credit freezes and fraud alerts](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/credit-freezes-and-fraud-alerts) | Free US freezes at all three nationwide bureaus; fraud-alert mechanism and scope. A freeze does not reverse misuse of an existing account. Live when checked. |
| [FTC, What to know about identity theft](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-identity-theft) | Types of misuse, signs, free recovery plan and report at IdentityTheft.gov. US only; page dated September 2024 and live when checked. |
| [Bank of Russia, Consumer-loan self-ban](https://www.cbr.ru/ckki/self-prohibition_credit/) | Russian self-ban since 2025, Gosuslugi/MFC routes, exclusions and existing-card limit. Cancellation requires a request and takes effect on the second calendar day after the cancellation record enters the credit history; it is not a US temporary freeze lift. Page listed last update 2026-06-02; live when checked. |
| [FTC, Use two-factor authentication](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/use-two-factor-authentication-protect-your-accounts) | SMS risk from SIM swap, stronger options where offered, SMS preferable to no second factor if it is the only option. Page dated September 2022; live when checked. |
| [NIST SP 800-63B rev. 4, Authenticators](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b/authenticators/) | Technical distinction: manually entered OTP is not phishing-resistant; WebAuthn/FIDO2 can bind authentication to a verifier name. Current standard page live when checked; service implementation and recovery still vary. |

Editorial corrections from the previous module: the first hour is a triage exercise, not a mandatory fixed order or assurance that containment is complete; a private browser window does not clean a device; no US three-bureau freeze is generalized to the UK/EU/Russia; an IC3 complaint does not perform a bank reversal; a public warning can be appropriate; and a clean scan or password change alone is not proof that an incident is closed.
